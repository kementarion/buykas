## 2026-06-30 Toccata Hard Fork — Covenants++ Upgrade

- On June 30, 2026 (DAA score 474,165,565), the Kaspa mainnet activated the Toccata hard fork.

- This is the biggest functional upgrade to Kaspa since the Crescendo upgrade (May 5, 2025). If Crescendo added lanes to the highway (10 BPS), Toccata adds intelligent traffic systems and advanced toll booths—turning it from a road that only handles fast traffic into a smart city highway capable of running all kinds of complex applications.


### (1). Covenants — "Smart Automatic Doors" (KIP-17 / KIP-20)

- Previously, Kaspa's transaction rules were simple—basic condition checks. Covenants enable much smarter rules:
  - e.g., "this money can only be spent after a certain time" or "must meet a specific condition to be transferred."
  - Supports multi-step complex flows, essentially a mini-contract chain at the base layer.

- KIP-17 extends the script engine's opcodes, forming the backbone of Covenants. KIP-20 introduces Covenant IDs for easy lineage tracking, making it simpler to manage contract relationships.

- Before you could only write "simple contracts," now you can write "smart conditional contracts" directly on Kaspa's L1, no extra L2 needed.


### (2). Native Assets — "Issue Tokens Directly on Kaspa"

- You can now issue and manage your own tokens directly on Kaspa L1 (similar to ERC-20 on Ethereum), but faster and cheaper.

- This opens the door for DeFi, game tokens, project tokens, and more. Kaspa is no longer just "Kaspa coin"—it can carry countless types of assets.


### (3). ZK Infrastructure (KIP-16 / KIP-21)

- KIP-16 adds dedicated ZK verifiers to Kaspa, supporting both Groth16 and RISC Zero zero-knowledge proof schemes. ZK proofs can now be verified directly on L1 without relying on external bridges or third parties.

- KIP-21 introduces Partitioned Sequencing Commitment, allowing ZK applications to only prove transactions relevant to themselves rather than the entire chain history. This dramatically reduces proof costs, paving the way for "ZK Rollups or applications based on Kaspa."

- You don't have to reveal everything to prove "I have permission" or "the calculation is correct"—and verification is fast.


### (4). Silverscript — "A Higher-Level Language for Developers"

- SilverScript is Kaspa's high-level smart contract language that compiles to native Kaspa Script. Developers can write complex Covenants with friendlier syntax instead of raw scripts.

- Now officially available after the hard fork.


### (5). Fee Adjustment — Minimum Fee Raised 100x

- The minimum fee went from 1 sompi/gram (1 sompi = 0.00000001 KAS) to 100 sompi/gram (~0.000001 KAS/gram). Gram is a unit of computational weight—simple transfers have low grams, complex transactions with Covenants or ZK have high grams. Fee = 100 sompi × max(computation grams, transaction bytes × 2), whichever is higher.

- The reason is that the new Covenants and ZK verification allow each transaction to carry more computation. The old standard was too low and could be abused to spam the network. This is about abuse prevention, not Kaspa becoming expensive.

- This is a node mempool policy rule, not a consensus-layer mandate. Zero-fee transactions are still valid in theory, but nodes will refuse to relay or mine them.

- For regular users: everyday small transfers are barely affected—Kaspa is still fast and cheap. Complex transactions involving Covenants or ZK will cost more, but that matches their actual resource consumption. Wallets and developers need to update fee estimation logic; the recommended approach is to use the RPC fee estimation API.

- Similar to how Ethereum and other chains adjusted their gas mechanisms after introducing smart contracts. Kaspa remains competitive as a fast, low-cost chain.


### (6). What This Upgrade Means

| Dimension | Before Toccata | After Toccata |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positioning** | Fast payment / digital cash | High-speed + programmable L1 infrastructure |
| **Asset Issuance** | KAS only | Native Tokens supported |
| **Contract Capability** | Basic transfer conditions | Covenants + ZK verification + advanced scripts |
| **Developer Entry** | Almost none | SilverScript + SDK + ZK toolchain |

- **For regular users**: Kaspa used to be fast, cheap, and secure "digital cash"—good for payments, transfers, and store of value. Now it can serve as cash AND run DeFi, NFTs, complex applications, and even advanced L2s in the future.

- **For developers**: You can build directly on Kaspa now, no need to wait for third-party L2 platforms.

- **For Kaspa itself**: This is the key step from "just fast" to "fast and smart." The core PoW + BlockDAG advantages remain, but the functional boundaries have expanded dramatically.

- In one sentence: Toccata turns Kaspa from "a good road" into "a road network where you can build cities."
